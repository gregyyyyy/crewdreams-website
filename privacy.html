Privacy policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

CrewDreams is an app for inflight rest and sleep planning for airline crews. This policy explains which data the app and this website process, why, and what your rights are. In short: there is no login and no CrewDreams server, only your flights are read, and everything stays on your device.

What the app stores – on your device only

  • No account. There is no login, no name, no e-mail address and no password.
  • Calendar link. If you connect your crew calendar, the link is stored on your device. Your phone loads the roster directly from your airline's calendar service when you open the app.
  • Flights from your roster. Flight numbers, airports, times, aircraft type, your position and your rest position (W, X, V). Sick days, leave and private entries are never read or stored.
  • Settings. Wake-up times, language and appearance.

None of this is sent to us. We have no server for the app and cannot see your data.

Deleting your data

“Remove link” in the profile deletes your calendar link and your roster from your device. Deleting the app removes everything.

Service providers

  • Apple and Google – distribution through the App Store, TestFlight and Google Play, under their own privacy policies.
  • GitHub (GitHub Inc., USA) – hosts this website and may log your IP address for security.

All providers act on our instructions under data processing agreements. Transfers to the USA are based on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

This website

This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no tracking. Your language choice is remembered in your browser only.

Your rights

You can ask for access to, correction or deletion of your data, restrict or object to processing, and receive your data in a portable format. Write to hello@crewdreams.com. You can also complain to a data protection authority, in Austria the Datenschutzbehörde (dsb.gv.at).

Was die App speichert – nur auf deinem Gerät

  • Kein Konto. Es gibt kein Login, keinen Namen, keine E-Mail-Adresse und kein Passwort.
  • Kalender-Link. Wenn du deinen Crew-Kalender verbindest, wird der Link auf deinem Gerät gespeichert. Dein Handy lädt den Plan beim Öffnen der App direkt vom Kalenderdienst deiner Airline.
  • Flüge aus deinem Plan. Flugnummern, Flughäfen, Zeiten, Flugzeugtyp, deine Funktion und deine Pausen-Position (W, X, V). Krankheitstage, Ferien und private Einträge werden nie gelesen oder gespeichert.
  • Einstellungen. Weckzeiten, Sprache und Darstellung.

Nichts davon wird an uns gesendet. Wir haben keinen Server für die App und können deine Daten nicht sehen.

Daten löschen

„Link entfernen“ im Profil löscht deinen Kalender-Link und deinen Plan von deinem Gerät. Wenn du die App löschst, ist alles weg.
